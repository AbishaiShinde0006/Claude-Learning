What is Research Mode?

Research mode is used when you need deep, detailed information on a complex topic. It is the most thorough of all search options — Claude conducts extensive research, synthesizes everything it finds, and delivers a comprehensive answer with citations.
Takes time: Expect 5–15 minutes for results. It is not for quick lookups.
When to use which tool:
ToolBest ForWeb SearchQuick, current, real-world informationEnterprise SearchInternal organisational knowledgeResearch ModeDeep, detailed research on complex topics
Example: You want to understand the EV market — who the major players are, supply chain challenges, and whether it's a viable investment. That's a research mode question, not a quick web search.
