Common AI Output Problems & Fixes

Confident but inaccurate — AI pulls from memory and assumes information. Fix: turn on web search so it pulls real-world data instead of guessing.
Wrong format — AI defaults to a professional, friendly tone and assumes structure. Fix: give it an example of exactly what you want, e.g. "write in 2 paragraphs, bullet points in bold."


The 4 D's Framework

Delegation — Decide what to hand off to Claude and what to keep for yourself.
Description — Give Claude clear context: who you are, what the task is, and what output you want.
Diligence — Don't treat AI as a one-shot tool. Think of your first prompt as the first sentence of a conversation — keep refining.
Discernment — Evaluate the output critically. Don't accept it blindly.


The Description–Discernment Loop
Keep cycling between describing what you want and evaluating what you get. Refine with each exchange until the output satisfies you.
If you've tried everything and still aren't getting the result you need — start a fresh conversation. Sometimes the context in a long chat works against you.
