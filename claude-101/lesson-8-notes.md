What are Connectors?
Connectors are integrations that link Claude to the external tools and data you actually use in your workflow. Instead of copy-pasting information into the chat, Claude accesses it directly.
Connectors can read, modify, or create data — depending on the level of permissions you grant.

2 Types of Connectors

Web Connectors — Connect Claude to cloud services like Google Drive, Gmail, or calendars. Claude can access, read, and modify files stored online.
Desktop Connectors — Connect Claude to files stored locally on your machine. Requires the Claude desktop app.


Key Advantage
Connectors give Claude access to your actual working data, not just information you manually paste into the chat.

Permissions
Claude only accesses what you explicitly allow. If you grant access to your inbox, it cannot access anyone else's — for example, it won't touch your manager's inbox. You stay in control of the boundaries.
