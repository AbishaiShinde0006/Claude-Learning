What is Enterprise Search?

Enterprise Search is a dedicated search feature that pulls answers only from the data sources your organisation has connected and loaded — meeting minutes, policy documents, contracts, guidelines, internal files. It does not search the web or any outside sources.
How it works:

When you ask a question, Claude searches across all connected organisational data, synthesizes the relevant information from multiple sources, and gives you one clean, coherent answer with citations — so you know exactly where the answer came from.
Key advantage:

You don't have to dig through documents manually. Claude does the cross-referencing and hands you a direct answer.
Example: You need to check the terms and conditions for a vendor contract but don't know which document it's in. Enterprise Search finds it, pulls the relevant section, and answers your question directly — without you opening a single file.
